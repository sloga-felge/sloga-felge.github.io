Why wheels take a beating in Tuzla

City driving is unforgiving. High kerbs around parking bays, sunken manhole covers and the raised edges left behind after roadworks all take their toll: one angled hit is enough to flatten a rim flange or knock the whole barrel out of true. Plenty of drivers only notice once the tyre starts losing air.

Here every wheel starts on the runout gauge. A buckled barrel goes onto the hydraulic press; where a piece of metal is missing or a crack has opened up, fresh material is welded in and the surplus is then ground back until the line of the edge returns. Alloy or steel, the sequence follows the damage.

Nothing leaves town. Straightening and welding both happen under one roof in Tuzla, so your wheel never goes on a trip of its own and you are not left waiting on transport. Diameters from small steel rims up to large low profile wheels are handled the same way.

Common questions from Tuzla

Where can I get buckled alloy wheels repaired in Tuzla?

At Servis felgi Sloga, 3. tuzlanske brigade 128, in the Simin Han district. Bent alloy rims are pressed back into shape and then measured again to see how much runout is left. Call or message +387 61 570 429 before you drive over.

Can I drop a wheel off without calling first?

It is better to get in touch on +387 61 570 429 first, because visits are arranged rather than tied to fixed hours. A short call or message settles it: you describe the damage, we say when to come, and the rim does not sit in your boot for days.

Do you work on steel wheels or only alloys?

Both are handled here. Steel rims are straightened and welded, and because they dent rather than crack, the press is the main tool for them. Alloys end up at the welder more often. The material decides the method, not whether the job gets done.

My steering wheel vibrates, is the rim to blame?

Very often it is. When a barrel runs out sideways or up and down, the shake comes through the steering at higher speeds, and balance weights do not cure it because the fault is in the shape. Bring the wheel in for a measurement; if it runs out, it is straightened on the press.

How do I know whether my wheel can be repaired at all?

Send a photo of the damage by WhatsApp or Viber to +387 61 570 429 and you get an assessment. Kerbed flanges, bead seats and cracks in the barrel are usually solvable. If a load bearing part is damaged and a safe repair is not possible, we say so openly.
